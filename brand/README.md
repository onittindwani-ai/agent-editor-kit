# Your brand profile lives here

This folder holds two files that your Claude Code writes for you when you run the setup interview
(`skills/ae-setup-interview`):

- `brand/profile.md`: your business, audience, offer, voice, script style, visual theme, captions, sound,
  platforms, rules and claims policy.
- `brand/theme.json`: your palette, fonts, background, corner radius and shadow.

Every skill in the kit reads these two files first. Nothing is written for you until they exist.

`profile.template.md` and `theme.template.json` show the exact shape. You do not fill them in by hand: say
"set up the Agent Editor Kit for me" and answer the questions.

Both of your files stay on your computer. They are listed in `.gitignore`, so they are not committed by accident.
