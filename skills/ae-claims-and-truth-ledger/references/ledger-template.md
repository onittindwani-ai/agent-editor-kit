## Fast row check (ask of every row)

1. What exactly is being counted or compared?
2. Which document says it, and which sentence of that document?
3. When was it written, and when did I read it?
4. What would a careful reader of that source conclude?
5. Is my wording the strongest sentence the source supports, and no stronger?
6. When does this stop being true?
