## Round 9: Platforms and formats (writes to `## Platforms and formats`)

Say first: "Round 9 of 12, where the videos will be posted and in what shape."

### Q9.1 Platforms. Pick all that apply. (writes: Platforms)
- A. Instagram Reels. B. YouTube Shorts. C. TikTok. D. LinkedIn video. E. YouTube long form (plus a website or other).
Ask for the account handle only if the user wants it shown on screen. Rule: the kit prepares posts; it never posts.

### Q9.2 Shape per platform. (writes: Formats per platform)
- A. Vertical 9:16 (1080 by 1920). Example: Reels, Shorts, TikTok.
- B. Square 1:1 (1080 by 1080). Example: feed posts.
- C. Portrait 4:5 (1080 by 1350). Example: feed video.
- D. Landscape 16:9 (1920 by 1080). Example: YouTube long form, LinkedIn.
Explain: a vertical cut and a landscape cut are different compositions, never one cropped from the other. Ask which version is the main one.

### Q9.3 Maximum length per platform. (writes: Max lengths)
Ask for the real limit the user wants to stay under; if unsure, say the platform limits change and record `verify at upload`. Do not state platform limits as facts.

### Q9.4 One cut or several? (writes: Cut variants)
- A. One cut for all platforms. B. Same story, slightly different cuts (for example different hook text). C. Separate compositions per shape.

### Q9.5 Phone for verification. (writes: Safe zone notes)
Ask which phone and which apps the user watches on, so the safe zones can be checked on a real screen. State that platform interface zones differ between apps and change over time; the kit's numbers are starting points the user must verify by screenshot.

### Q9.6 Posting cadence (informational). (writes: Cadence)
- A. Daily. B. 3 to 5 a week. C. Weekly. D. Irregular. Used only to size plans; never to schedule or post.

### Q9.7 Who posts? (writes: Posting handled by)
- A. The user, by hand. B. A teammate. C. A scheduler the user controls. Rule: nothing is published by the kit.

## Round 10: House rules on words and visuals (writes: Rules > House rules)

Say first: "Round 10 of 12, your standing rules. Anything you say here becomes a check that every skill respects."

### Q10.1 Punctuation and style rules. (writes: House rules)
- A. No dashes of any kind in text that viewers read (use commas, periods, colons, parentheses). Example: "Two steps: mix, then rest."
- B. No exclamation marks. C. No emojis. D. No ALL CAPS words except keywords. E. None of these.
Allow several. Tell the user the kit scans scripts and on screen text for banned punctuation before delivery.

### Q10.2 Words and topics to avoid. (writes: Banned words)
Offer a starter list and let them edit: "game changer", "secret", "insane", "you will not believe", "unlock", "hack". Ask for topics never to touch (for example politics, named competitors).

### Q10.3 Identity on screen. (writes: Identity on visuals)
- A. No name, handle or role label on any graphic. B. Handle only on the end card. C. Name and role on the first lower third only. D. User decides per video.

### Q10.4 Cut style. (writes: Cut style)
- A. Tight: remove every pause of 0.25 seconds or more, fillers and breaths. Example: fast, punchy talk.
- B. Natural: remove pauses over 0.4 seconds and clear fillers, keep breaths. Example: friendly teaching.
- C. Relaxed: remove only mistakes and long dead air. Example: reflective or long form.

### Q10.5 Fillers and restarts. (writes: Filler policy)
- A. Remove all fillers ("um", "uh", "you know") and restarts. B. Remove restarts, keep the user's natural "okay so". C. Keep everything except mistakes.

### Q10.6 Reuse of other people's material. (writes: Third party material)
- A. Never use anyone else's footage, music, logos or screenshots without a recorded permission or licence. B. Short screenshots of public pages are fine when the source is shown and the edit adds commentary (the user takes responsibility; the kit records the source). C. Ask each time.
Say: this is not legal advice.

### Q10.7 Effects and motion taste. (writes: Motion taste)
- A. Almost none, mostly cuts and captions. B. Subtle: soft entrances, one punch zoom per major point. C. Lively: several graphics and zooms, still readable.
Mention the editor's limits: 4 to 6 punch zooms suggested, at least 4 seconds apart; at most 10 graphics.

## Round 11: Claims policy (writes to `## Claims policy`)

Say first: "Round 11 of 12, how careful agents should be with facts, numbers and promises."

### Q11.1 Strictness. (writes: Strictness)
- A. Strict: every number, name, date and comparison needs a source in the ledger before it is spoken or shown.
- B. Standard: numbers, names, dates and any comparison need a source; plain advice does not.
- C. Light: only anything that could harm someone or that sounds like a promise needs a source.
Recommend A or B. If the user picks C for a regulated topic (health, money, law), say that you recommend B.

### Q11.2 What counts as acceptable evidence? (writes: Evidence classes)
- A. Primary sources (the official page, a dataset, a document the user holds). B. The user's own first hand experience, labelled as such. C. Reputable secondary reports, labelled as secondary. D. Vendor claims, labelled as the vendor's claim.
Allow several. Never allow "everyone knows".

### Q11.3 Results and testimonials. (writes: Results and testimonials)
- A. Never show or state a result without proof the user can show. B. Real, permitted customer results only, with consent noted. C. None at all.
Rule: no invented results, no composite customers, no results phrased as typical.

### Q11.4 Regulated topics. (writes: Regulated topics)
Ask whether the videos touch health, money and investing, law, children, safety or security. For each yes, ask for the disclaimer wording the user wants (for example "general information, not advice") and where it appears (spoken, on screen, caption).

### Q11.5 Dates and freshness. (writes: Date handling)
- A. Say when an event happened ("in March last year"). B. Avoid dates that will age. C. Always show the date on screen.
Rule: volatile facts (prices, versions, rules) are rechecked on filming day and posting day.

### Q11.6 Naming other people and companies. (writes: Third party mentions)
- A. Only neutral, factual mentions with a source. B. Never name anyone. C. Praise only. D. Ask each time.
Rule: no claims about the character or private life of any person.

### Q11.7 Words that sound like promises. (writes: Never claim)
Offer a starter list: "guaranteed", "always works", "100 percent", "risk free", "cure", "get rich". Ask for their own additions.

## Round 12: Delivery gates and working setup (writes: Rules > Gates, Working setup)

Say first: "Last round, 12 of 12, how we work together and what needs your yes."

### Q12.1 Script approval. (writes: Gate script approval)
- A. I approve the exact word for word script before anything else is built (recommended). B. I approve an outline, agents write the words. C. I supply the script myself and it is final.
Default: A. State: for A and C, agents never reword approved lines; changes go through a suggestion list.

### Q12.2 Export gate. (writes: Gate export)
- A. Nothing is rendered to a final file until I say "export". Reviews use stills and the live preview. (default)
- B. Draft renders are allowed for review, final export still needs my yes.
Mention: B costs time and disk, so recommend A.

### Q12.3 Publishing gate. (writes: Gate publish)
Fixed: the kit never publishes, schedules, comments or messages anyone. Ask only whether the user wants post copy prepared (titles, descriptions, captions, chapters) at the end.

### Q12.4 Notes handling. (writes: Notes policy)
- A. Batch my notes, fix them all, show stills. B. Fix each note at once. State: every note also becomes a rule or a check (`ae-owner-notes-to-rules`).

### Q12.5 Computer limits. (writes: Laptop limits)
- A. Light laptop: one heavy job at a time, 2 threads. B. Normal laptop: one heavy job at a time, 4 threads. C. Powerful desktop: 8 threads, still one heavy job at a time.
Ask how much free disk space to keep (default 5 GB) and whether the machine is shared.

### Q12.6 Folders. (writes: Working setup)
Show the recommended layout (`footage/`, `scripts/`, `edit/`, `exports/`, `brand/`, `agent-editor/`) and ask whether to keep it or name different folders. Ask where raw takes live and say they are never edited in place.

### Q12.7 Deleting and cleanup. (writes: Cleanup policy)
- A. Move superseded renders to the system Trash after each verified version. B. Keep everything until I say. C. Ask each time. State: deletion only ever uses literal absolute paths and the Trash; never wildcards.

### Q12.8 Credits and attribution. (writes: Credits)
Ask whether any music, sound or footage requires an on screen or description credit, and where credits should be listed.

After Q12.8, go to the confirm step in `ae-setup-interview` section 7.
